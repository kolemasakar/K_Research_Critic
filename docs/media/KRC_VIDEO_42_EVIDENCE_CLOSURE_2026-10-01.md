## Source hierarchy and status definitions
A = primary controlled human experiment or government safety recommendation matching the claim; B = experimental food-chemistry/animal/in-vitro evidence matching a narrower mechanism; C = reputable culinary demonstration/manufacturer instructions or mechanistic plausibility, not proof of a universal claim; D = no identified independent source for exact figure or universal language. 'Contradicted' means source evidence opposes the absolute formulation, not that the entire recipe fails.
