## Verified items

### #10 Red kidney beans — SUPPORTED, important safety qualification
FDA confirms phytohaemagglutinin poisoning and that as few as 4–5 raw beans can trigger symptoms. FDA consumer guidance: soak at least 5 h, discard soaking water and boil in fresh water at least **30 minutes**. Do not substitute '10 minutes' as sufficient universal cooking advice; undercooked beans remain unsafe and slow cookers can be unsuitable.
Sources: https://www.fda.gov/files/food/published/Bad-Bug-Book-2nd-Edition-%28PDF%29.pdf ; https://www.fda.gov/food/chemical-contaminants-pesticides/natural-toxins-food

### #32 Pasteurized milk — SUPPORTED under storage/product conditions
CDC: properly pasteurized milk is preferred over raw milk, and pasteurization kills harmful pathogens. Routine re-boiling of commercially pasteurized milk is not required merely to pasteurize it again, **provided** packaging, shelf life, refrigeration and handling are appropriate. This does not make spoiled/improperly stored pasteurized milk safe. The video's raw-milk discussion must not be taken as endorsing raw milk.
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/raw-milk.html

### #2 Broccoli/sulforaphane — PARTIALLY SUPPORTED; headline overstates
2008 crossover study in eight men found sulforaphane bioavailability **37% raw versus 3.4% cooked**, close to figures in the video. This is a specific study on sulforaphane, not evidence that cooked broccoli loses all or its 'main' nutritional value. Cooking duration and method matter: 2017 in-vitro digestion study found raw and 1-minute steamed broccoli up to 10x higher sulforaphane formation than longer steaming; other cultivar work found 1–3-minute steaming can improve sulforaphane yield under some conditions. Thus blanket 'up to 5 min preserves enzyme' or 'microwave >1 min always destroys most' should not be treated as universal without matched method and conditions.
Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18950181/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27507513/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22471240/
