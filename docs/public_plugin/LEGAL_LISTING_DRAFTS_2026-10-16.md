# KRC Public — listing page drafts (NOT PUBLIC, require owner review)

## Website
K-Research & Critic — structured evidence review and research quality checks. Does not autonomously establish facts without sources. The public research MCP processes input provided by the user and returns profile validations, source-independence checks and report consistency findings. No video or social-media access.

## Privacy Policy — proposed
K-Research & Critic Public Research MCP processes only the profile, claims, evidence metadata and report text that you explicitly supply to a tool. It uses this information to validate evidence quality and report structure. It does not access your accounts, social media, video links, payment details or unrelated conversation data. The proposed MCP does not persist research submissions in an application database, and should not record their contents in service logs. Hosting/platform operational logs may contain request metadata; retention period and processors must be verified against actual hosting before publication. The plugin is not designed to receive sensitive personal data. Do not submit personal documents, private credentials or confidential evidence. The privacy and deletion contact must be specified before publication. This text MUST be reconciled with the live server implementation and hosting before being claimed as policy.

## Terms of Service — proposed
This plugin provides research quality-control assistance, not certification of factual accuracy or professional legal/medical/financial advice. Users are responsible for verifying primary sources and making their own decisions. No guarantee is made that the tool has identified all inconsistencies. Do not submit unlawful, confidential or third-party restricted information without authorization. Access may be rate-limited for safety and sustainability. Operator identity, governing jurisdiction, support contact and any required legal terms MUST be reviewed by the owner before publication.
