## Working title
Evidence boundaries in long-cycle silicon–carbon anodes: distinctions between half-cell durability, full-cell results and independent reproducibility

## Abstract (structured — illustrative)
**Background.** Silicon–carbon (Si–C) anodes are proposed to improve lithium-ion energy density; durability claims vary across cell formats.
**Objective.** Evaluate the boundaries of claims of more than 1000 cycles at >=80% retained capacity.
**Methods.** This demonstration reuses the limited owner-provided KRC evidence ledger, with source identifiers S1–S8 and experiment origins O1–O4. Inclusion requires a source-reported >1000 cycles, retained capacity >=80% against the reported baseline, identified test protocol, and accessible methods/results for a narrow factual assertion. Abstract-only and metadata-only sources are excluded from independent experimental verification. No new database search or PRISMA-compliant systematic screening was done for this sample.
**Results.** The inherited ledger describes S1 (half-cell: around 1500 cycles, >87% after formation) and two distinct full-cell reports S2 (reported >1000/>80% under the described test) and S3 (1470 cycles to 80% in full-cell). S4 uses capacity limiting and is ineligible for this retention threshold. Claims of independent interlaboratory replication of the identical recipe remain unsupported. These values are attributed to the source report, **not newly independently verified here**.
**Conclusion.** The scoped examples suggest technical feasibility for some configurations but do not establish universal or commercially reproducible long-term durability.

## Discussion
Transfer of anode material results to full-cell life is invalid without controlled matching. The report itself flags the conflict between a narrative >1500-cycle claim and a shorter displayed secondary trace for S2, and between introductory 1500 and more specific 1470 at 80% for S3. The value of the present paper, if completed, would be an auditable exclusion ledger and operational cross-study comparability rubric. **These are methodological proposals, not demonstrated original scientific findings.**
Limits: unverified independent source reread; incomplete search; no original raw data or survival distribution; unknown statistical repeat count; no independent coauthor critique; no manuscript originality test.

## Conclusions
Selected published experiments, as described by the preceding KRC analysis, report >1000 cycles and >=80% in certain anode and full-cell contexts. No evidence here supports a universal promise for all Si–C cells. The main manuscript remains a working draft pending primary-source reinspection, comprehensive screening where applicable, journal selection and author signoff.
